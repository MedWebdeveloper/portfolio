Staking explained: how to earn passive income with ZNT

🔒

If you're holding crypto and letting it sit idle in a wallet, you're leaving money on the table. Staking is one of the simplest ways to earn a return on your tokens — and with Zenith, you can earn up to 18% APY. Here's exactly how it works.

What is staking, really?

At its core, staking means committing your tokens to support the operation of a network or protocol. In return, you receive rewards — a bit like earning interest in a savings account, except the rates are typically much higher and everything happens transparently on-chain.

Think of staking as putting your tokens to work while you sleep.

Flexible vs. locked staking

Zenith offers two staking modes, and choosing the right one depends on your goals.

Flexible staking

Your tokens stay liquid — you can unstake and withdraw at any time. The trade-off is a lower APY (currently around 6%). This is ideal if you might need access to your funds on short notice.

Locked staking

You commit your tokens for a fixed period (30, 90 or 180 days). In exchange, you earn a significantly higher APY, up to 18% for the longest lock. The longer you commit, the more you earn.

The magic of compounding

Zenith staking rewards auto-compound, meaning your earned rewards are automatically added to your staked balance and start earning too. Over time, compounding dramatically increases your total return.

  • Stake 3,000 ZNT at 18% APY.
  • Rewards compound automatically, no manual claiming needed.
  • After one year, you hold significantly more than a simple 18% would suggest.

How to start staking in 3 steps

Getting started takes less than two minutes on the Zenith dashboard:

  • Connect your wallet and make sure you hold some ZNT.
  • Choose flexible or a locked term that fits your timeline.
  • Confirm the transaction — your rewards start accruing immediately.

A word on risk

Staking is relatively low-risk compared to active trading, but it isn't risk-free. Locked staking means you can't access funds until the term ends, and token prices can still fluctuate. Only stake what fits your strategy, and always understand the terms before committing.
