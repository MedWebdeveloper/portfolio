FAQ

Questions, answered

What is Zenith (ZNT)?+
Zenith is a decentralized finance protocol and its native utility token, ZNT. It powers swaps, staking, governance and cross-chain transfers within the Zenith ecosystem.
How do I buy ZNT?+
You can buy ZNT directly in our dashboard, or on major exchanges. Connect your wallet, swap ETH/USDT for ZNT, and you're ready to stake or trade.
Is Zenith audited?+
Yes. All smart contracts are audited by CertiK, and we maintain a $10M insurance fund plus a public bug-bounty program.
What returns can I earn from staking?+
Staking APY ranges from 6% (flexible) up to 18% (locked pools), with rewards auto-compounding and distributed on-chain.
