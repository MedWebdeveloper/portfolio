📋 Context

FinBank is an established digital bank with 2.5 million users. Despite a solid foundation, the mobile app suffered from an outdated user experience, leading to decreased engagement and increased customer complaints. Management decided to invest in a complete redesign to remain competitive against emerging neo-banks.

"I gave up three times before successfully creating my account." - Thomas, 28 years old
03

Outdated Design

Dated interface that doesn't meet modern standards. Lack of visual consistency and clear hierarchy.

"The app looks like something from 2010. Not very reassuring." - Sophie, 42 years old

Lack of Personalization

Generic experience that doesn't adapt to individual needs. No personalized recommendations or insights.

🔑 Key Design Decisions

1. Simplified Navigation

Decision: Reduce from 7 to 4 main tabs

Why: 78% of users couldn't find essential features

Impact: Navigation time reduced by 65%

2. Progressive Onboarding

Decision: From 15 to 4 essential steps

Why: 45% abandonment during registration

Impact: Completion rate increased to 88%

3. Personalized Dashboard

Decision: Create adaptive dashboard based on usage

Why: Each user has different needs

Impact: Engagement increased by 240%

4. Quick Actions

Decision: Add shortcuts for 5 most frequent actions

Why: 80% of actions are repetitive

Impact: Average task time reduced by 60%

📚 Key Learnings

01

Research is Essential

Investing time in upfront user research helped avoid costly mistakes and create a truly adapted solution. The 6 weeks of research were crucial for project success.

02

Simplicity > Features

We learned that having fewer but better-executed features is preferable to an overloaded app. Navigation simplification had the greatest impact on satisfaction.

03

Test Early and Often

Regular user testing allowed us to identify and fix problems quickly. Each testing cycle brought valuable insights that improved the final design.

04

Accessibility Benefits Everyone

By designing for accessibility from the start, we created a better experience for all users, not just those with specific needs.

05

Data Guides Decisions

Combining quantitative data (analytics) and qualitative (interviews) gave us a complete vision and helped us prioritize the right features.

06

Collaboration is Key

Working closely with developers, product management, and business created a technically viable solution aligned with business objectives.

🎯 Conclusion

This FinBank redesign project demonstrates the impact that a user-centered UX approach can have on product success. By investing in research, constantly iterating, and staying focused on real user needs, we transformed an outdated banking app into a modern, intuitive, and engaging experience.

The results speak for themselves: +333% active users, App Store rating from 2.8★ to 4.8★, and a 66% reduction in customer support calls. But beyond the numbers, we created an app that users love to use daily.

This project allowed me to strengthen my skills in user research, interface design, and cross-functional collaboration. I learned the importance of simplicity, accessibility, and continuous iteration in creating successful digital products.
