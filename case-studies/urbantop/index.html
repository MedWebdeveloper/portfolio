Bundle Experience

A Shopify storefront and bundle flow built around a simple conversion goal: make the “Buy 3, Get 1 Free” offer immediately understandable and carry that incentive from discovery through product selection and cart.

ShopifyBundle OfferProduct VariantsAutomatic DiscountResponsive UX

The challenge

Design a clear e-commerce journey that encourages shoppers to build a four-item bundle instead of stopping after one product. The experience needed to communicate the promotion clearly, support color and size choices, and keep the path to checkout simple.

My role

I built and configured the Shopify storefront, structured the bundle collection, created product variants and size selection, configured the promotional logic, and tested the customer journey from landing page through cart and checkout.

Conversion approach

01

Lead with value

The hero puts “BUY 3, GET 1 FREE” directly under the main headline with a clear Build Your Bundle CTA.

02

Reduce choice friction

Four colorways are presented together and shoppers can mix colors and sizes.

03

Simple variants

Size choices 7–12 are visible in the product quick view before adding an item to cart.

04

Reward completion

Once four qualifying products are in the cart, the bundle promotion automatically makes one pair free.

What I focused on

Clear promotional messaging, product discoverability, simple size selection, consistent bundle language and a cart state that visibly confirms the reward.

Next CRO tests

For a production campaign, I would test a live bundle-progress message such as “Add 1 more to unlock your free pair,” a sticky mobile bundle CTA, placement of genuine customer reviews, and alternative CTA copy.
